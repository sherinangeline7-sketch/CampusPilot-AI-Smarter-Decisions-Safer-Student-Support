Title:
CampusPilot AI: Smarter Decisions, Safer Student Support

Description (max 512 characters):
CampusPilot AI is an AI-powered student support agent that answers campus questions, helps students handle timetable conflicts, and creates placement preparation plans. Strands Decider checks requests before the agent responds and routes sensitive matters, such as medical leave and grade disputes, to the appropriate human authority. Built with Amazon Bedrock AgentCore Runtime.
